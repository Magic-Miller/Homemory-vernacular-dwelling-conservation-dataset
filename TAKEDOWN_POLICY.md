# Correction, Restriction, and Takedown Policy

## Purpose

The project accepts good-faith requests concerning copyright, privacy, consent, cultural sensitivity, inaccurate attribution, sensitive locations, and other potential harms associated with released or controlled material.

## Who May Submit a Request

A request may be submitted by a contributor, copyright holder, property owner, occupant, depicted person, authorized representative, community authority, or another person directly affected by the material.

## Information Needed

Where safely possible, a request should provide:

- the relevant case, image, record, or release identifier;
- the requester's relationship to the material;
- the requested action: correction, attribution change, redaction, location restriction, access restriction, or removal;
- a concise explanation and any supporting evidence;
- a private method for the project to respond.

Sensitive evidence must not be posted in a public GitHub issue. The project must publish a private contact route before public data collection or release begins.

## Review and Interim Action

The project will assess identity and authority, urgency, possible harm, consent and rights records, public-interest considerations, and the technical feasibility of the request. Where credible risk is identified, maintainers may temporarily restrict access while the review is pending.

## Possible Outcomes

The project may:

- correct metadata or attribution;
- redact an image or generalize a location;
- restrict a record or supporting material;
- remove material from the current hosted release where practicable;
- exclude the material from future releases;
- publish a correction or withdrawal notice without repeating sensitive information;
- decline a request with a documented reason when appropriate.

## Limits After Public Release

The project can change material under its control but cannot guarantee deletion of copies already downloaded, mirrored, redistributed, or incorporated into downstream work. Removal from a later release does not revoke CC BY 4.0 permissions already received by compliant users of an earlier release.

## Recordkeeping

Decisions and actions should be recorded privately with the request date, affected identifiers, reviewer, outcome, release impact, and any required follow-up. Public release notes should describe material changes without exposing private information.

# Data Contents

## Scope

The dataset concerns historic vernacular dwellings and closely related residential structures whose local materials, building traditions, social meaning, age, or conservation needs make them relevant to the project. Final eligibility criteria will be tested during the pilot phase.

## Core Data

The core release unit is a building case containing one or more photographs. Core records are expected to include:

- stable building-case and image identifiers;
- photographs suitable for documentation or defect annotation;
- general location at an approved level of precision;
- building type and photographed component where known;
- material information where observable;
- visible defect labels and spatial annotations where applicable;
- annotation provenance, confidence, and review status;
- consent, rights, and privacy status.

## Encouraged Context

Contributors are encouraged, but not required, to provide:

- building history and approximate age;
- current and former use;
- alterations and maintenance history;
- family memories, community relationships, and historical stories;
- local terminology and traditional building knowledge.

Missing contextual stories do not make an otherwise valid image contribution ineligible.

## Expert-Reviewed Knowledge

Where available, qualified reviewers may add:

- possible causes and alternative interpretations;
- risk notes and indicators for escalation;
- suggested inspection priorities;
- conservation principles;
- discouraged interventions;
- maintenance considerations and references.

Interpretive knowledge must be separated from directly observable evidence and must record authorship, review status, uncertainty, and date.

## Proposed Completeness Levels

- **Level 1 — Field record:** images, consent, and basic case information.
- **Level 2 — Annotated record:** visible defects and relevant components or materials annotated.
- **Level 3 — Reviewed record:** annotations checked by a qualified reviewer.
- **Level 4 — Knowledge-enriched record:** reviewed conservation knowledge added.

Public releases may contain records at different levels, but the level must be explicit.

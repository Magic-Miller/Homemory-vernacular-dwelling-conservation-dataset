# Ethics and Privacy

## Principles

The project prioritizes informed participation, respect for occupants and communities, minimal disclosure of sensitive information, and conservation benefit over unrestricted data collection.

## Permission and Consent

Contributors must have permission to take and submit photographs. Private interiors, occupied homes, restricted properties, and community-controlled places may require additional owner, occupant, custodian, or community consent.

Contributors document permission by completing the [Contribution and Consent Form](../CONTRIBUTION_AND_CONSENT.txt) and emailing it with the submission. The form records permission for public display, dataset redistribution, AI training, commercial use, attribution, and approved location precision. Completed forms and private evidence remain outside the public repository and dataset.

## Personal Information

Before public release, images and metadata must be checked for:

- recognizable faces;
- vehicle license plates;
- house numbers and precise addresses;
- personal documents and family photographs;
- private belongings or activities;
- embedded camera metadata that reveals sensitive location information.

Sensitive material should be removed, redacted, generalized, restricted, or rejected according to the approved policy.

## Location Sensitivity

Exact locations may expose private households, vulnerable buildings, sacred places, or sites at risk of theft, vandalism, tourism pressure, or unauthorized entry. Public location precision must be decided case by case and recorded in the metadata.

## Cultural and Community Context

Legal permission alone may not be sufficient for culturally sensitive knowledge or places. Contributors and maintainers should consider community expectations, customary authority, and whether publication could cause harm or misrepresentation.

## Stories and Oral Histories

Personal stories are optional. Contributors must not share another person's identifiable story without appropriate permission. Editing, translation, and anonymization must preserve meaning and record provenance.

## Withdrawal and Correction

A private route for correction, redaction, restriction, and takedown requests must be established before public launch. The policy must explain what can be changed in future releases and what limitations apply to data already downloaded by third parties.

## AI Use

AI-generated labels or interpretations must be disclosed and reviewed. Models trained on the dataset must not be presented as providing definitive structural diagnosis or site-specific repair instructions without appropriate professional evaluation.

## Attribution

Users should provide reasonable dataset-level attribution that includes:

- the dataset title;
- the credited creator, contributor, or project attribution listed in the release metadata;
- the exact dataset release or version;
- a link to the dataset release when practicable;
- a link to the CC BY 4.0 license;
- an indication of whether material was modified.

A recommended form is:

> Open Dataset for Historic Vernacular Dwelling Conservation contributors, release [version], licensed under CC BY 4.0. Changes were made where applicable.

When a record identifies a specific photographer or contributor who requested attribution, users should retain that credit in a reasonable manner. Attribution must not imply endorsement by the contributor, property owner, community, reviewer, or project.

## Materials Not Automatically Covered

The public data license does not automatically cover:

- unreviewed submissions or controlled intake materials;
- private consent evidence, contact information, or original restricted metadata;
- exact locations withheld for privacy, safety, or conservation reasons;
- third-party material that the project is not authorized to license;
- building stories, oral histories, family memories, or culturally sensitive knowledge unless the record explicitly confirms public release under CC BY 4.0;
- items marked as restricted, withdrawn, or governed by a separate rights notice.

Only material expressly included in an approved public release is offered under this data license.

CONTRIBUTION AND CONSENT FORM
Open Dataset for Historic Vernacular Dwelling Conservation
Form version: 1.0 (2026-08-16)

HOW TO USE THIS FORM

1. Download this text file.
2. Type your answers in the blank spaces and replace [ ] with [X] for your choices.
3. Normally complete one form for one building case. Complete another form if the
   photographs have different creators, permissions, attribution choices, or location restrictions.
4. Email the completed form together with the original photographs. Follow the email
   instructions in CONTRIBUTING.md.
5. Do not upload a completed form, signature, contact details, or permission evidence to GitHub.
